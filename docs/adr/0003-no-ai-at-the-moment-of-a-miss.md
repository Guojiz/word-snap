# No AI at the moment of a miss

At a **Miss** the app shows only what a table lookup gives (a **Root breakdown** or **Near-synonym note**), never AI output, because an on-device model takes about 10 seconds per answer on a recent phone and the **Board** must never wait. **AI mnemonics** for missed words are queued in the background, one at a time, and appear on the **Set summary** and in the **Library**.
